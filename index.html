OUR STORY

BUILT AROUND
fire, flavor
AND THE TABLE.

EMBER & OLIVE brings together seasonal ingredients, open-fire cooking, and thoughtful hospitality in a space designed for long evenings and memorable conversations.

Our open hearth kitchen celebrates elemental cooking techniques, elevating locally sourced Indian produce alongside premium international ingredients.

ATMOSPHERE & CRAFT

The Experience

01 // THE KITCHEN

Open-fire cooking, seasonal produce, and ingredients treated with respect.

Our open hearth kitchen is the heart of Ember & Olive. Watching raw ingredients transform over wood embers creates a sensory connection between chef and guest.

02 // THE CELLAR

A thoughtful collection of wines, cocktails, and spirits selected to complement the menu.

From small-batch Old World vintages to smoked botanical cocktails, our beverage program is designed to elevate every dish on the menu.

03 // THE ROOM

Warm light, natural textures, and an atmosphere designed for staying a little longer.

Intimate booth seating, acoustic warmth, and ambient candle illumination set the stage for long conversations and special celebrations.

MEET THE CHEF

Arjun Mehra

Executive Culinary Director

"Chef Arjun Mehra approaches contemporary cooking with a simple philosophy: respect the ingredient, master the fire, and let the season lead."

Having honed his culinary techniques across Michelin-starred kitchens in Europe and Asia, Arjun returned to Kolkata to build Ember & Olive — celebrating elemental fire cooking with local Indian produce.
